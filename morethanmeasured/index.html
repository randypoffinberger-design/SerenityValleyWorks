Free account

Create an account and verify your email. Create your family's household, or join an existing household using an invitation. Joining uses that household's existing access.

Household access

Eligible households can start a free 7-day trial, with no card required and no automatic charge. Afterward, choose $12.99/month or $99.99/year. One household membership covers you and your spouse.

Babysitter registration is free, with parent-approved shared access. Subscribing starts paid access immediately; you can wait until the trial ends. Saved data is preserved when access ends and can be exported.
