MORE THAN MEASURED

Resource Library

Practical starting points for everyday caregiving. These guides are educational, not medical advice, and are designed to help families notice patterns, prepare questions and find approaches worth trying with their own child.

Sleep

Building a calmer bedtime routine

Use predictable steps, sensory clues and simple tracking to learn what helps bedtime go more smoothly.

Daily living

Potty learning without turning it into a battle

Readiness, routine, communication and cleanup strategies for a process that rarely follows a perfect schedule.

Sensory

Choosing sensory supports by need, not trend

Start with the situation and the child's response before buying a basket full of sensory products.

Communication

Supporting communication beyond spoken words

Make room for gestures, signs, pictures, devices and speech while focusing on meaningful communication.

Meals

Making mealtimes more predictable

Reduce surprises, observe food patterns and separate exposure from pressure when possible.

Routines

When routines help and when they need flexibility

Create useful anchors for the day without making one unexpected change derail everything.

Safety

Building a family safety plan

Think through identification, wandering, communication, emergency contacts and caregiver handoffs before a stressful moment.

Outings

Preparing for outings and travel

Preview the day, pack for regulation and create an exit plan that doesn't treat leaving early as failure.

Play

Using interests to support play and learning

Start from what already holds attention and build connection, language and new experiences around it.

Caregivers

Organizing the information everyone needs

Keep routines, medications, contacts, preferences and useful observations understandable to another caregiver.
